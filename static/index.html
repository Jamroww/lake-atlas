Vintage park style recipe
  1. Start from Outdoors. In Mapbox Studio, create a new style from Outdoors.
  2. Recolor the paper. Land #E7DFCB, water #9FB7AA, parks #C9C39E, roads #B8A78A, labels #2C352C.
  3. Keep the relief. Retain contour and hillshade layers. Warm the hillshade shadow to #6B5D45 and lower its opacity to about 18%.
  4. Publish and copy. Use Share → Style URL, paste the mapbox://styles/… value here, then Reconnect map.

The app adds its own paper wash, cream control chrome, terrain source, and analysis layers. Your Studio style supplies the land, water, roads, labels, and topographic linework.

Aeration siting uses the single deepest sounding. The herb buffer is a Turf.js ring; it is a planning overlay, not a legal setback. Field-verify utilities and permit requirements before planting or installing equipment.
